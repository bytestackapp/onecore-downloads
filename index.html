Install in three steps

It takes about a minute on your school device.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap Download for Android. If your browser warns about the file type, choose Keep.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the downloaded file. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.

  3. Open and sign in

    Launch OneCore and sign in with your school account.
