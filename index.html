Install in three steps

  1. Download the APK

    Tap Download APK on your school device. If your browser warns about the file type, choose Keep.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the downloaded file. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.

  3. Open and sign in

    Launch OneCore and sign in with your school account.

You need an account from your school to sign in. Only install OneCore from this page.
